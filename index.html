Manmatha Roy

Associate Scientist, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata

I am an Associate Scientist at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, where I am also pursuing my PhD under the joint supervision of Subhamoy Maitra and Arijit Ghosh.

My work is in theoretical computer science, with a particular focus on Boolean functions. I study their structural properties — especially their Fourier spectrum — and use that structure to design algorithms for problems in cryptography, additive combinatorics and learning theory. A recurring theme in my work is that simplicity in the spectral structure acts as a simplifying principle: where the spectrum is sparse or low in norm, testing and learning become tractable.

Before joining ISI Kolkata, I spent about two years at the Centre for Distributed Computing, Jadavpur University, working on the design and development of critical software systems.
