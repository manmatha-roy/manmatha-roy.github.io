Manmatha Roy

Associate Scientist, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata

I am an Associate Scientist at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, where I am also pursuing my PhD under the joint supervision of Subhamoy Maitra and Arijit Ghosh.

My research is in theoretical computer science, with a focus on understanding how structure can make complex computational problems easier to solve. I study structural properties such as sparsity and invariance to develop efficient algorithms for learning, testing, and verification. More recently, I have been exploring these ideas in the context of trustworthy AI, including efficient auditing of model properties, structured representations of complex models, and cryptographic techniques for providing independently verifiable guarantees about AI systems.

Besides my research activities, I am also part of the Central IT Team at ISI Kolkata, where I am responsible for the management of enterprise IT infrastructure, with a particular focus on IT security management.

Before joining ISI Kolkata, I spent a couple of years as a Research Engineer at the Centre for Distributed Computing, where I worked on the design and development of mission-critical software systems for the defence sector.

See my research and publications and the courses I have taught at ISI.
